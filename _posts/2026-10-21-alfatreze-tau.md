TAU music player for the Analogue Pocket. It plays MP3 and FLAC from the SD card: the music, covers and library index must be put there with Tau Omega (the companion app) or with the Python tools in the project repository (tools/sync_media.py). https://github.com/alfatreze/Tau-Alpha
