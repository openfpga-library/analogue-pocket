SkyRoads (c) 1993 Bluemoon Interactive, freeware: the full game is a free
download from http://www.bluemoon.ee/history/skyroads/index.html

The game's own code, from the decompilation at https://skyroads.org,
compiled for a RISC-V CPU that runs in the FPGA, with the VGA screen, the
AdLib (an OPL2), the Sound Blaster's samples and the PC speaker as hardware
next to it.  The game's files are included.

Controls: d-pad steer / accelerate / brake; A, B or Y jump (Enter in the
menus); Start Enter; X or Select Esc.
