MooBucky 0.4.1 by mmmax4
Moo Mesa / Bucky O'Hare, World EAB

D-pad: move; A/Y: jump; B: shoot.
X: Bucky button 3 / Moo alternate shoot.
Select: coin; Start: start.
DIP: Sound, Coins and Players in Core Settings.
Test/Service buttons: assign P1 keys in Core Settings.
Test Switch (hold): manual Test menu entry.
EEPROM saves cabinet settings; RAM high scores are not saved.
No ROMs or saves included.

Based on plasticbugs/Scott, Jotego, Marcus Andrade/OpenGateware,
FX68K, tv80, MAME, Analogue and all original contributors.
