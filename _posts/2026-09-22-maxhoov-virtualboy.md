Virtual Boy - experimental Pocket port
Original Virtual Boy RTL: Jamie Blanks.
384x224 native-rate video; selectable eye/anaglyph display.
8 KiB cartridge SRAM via openFPGA data slot 1.
Use a dock controller for complete dual-pad controls.
Tested on Pocket firmware 2.7; menu pauses game and audio.
No savestates, sleep, cheats or link support.
