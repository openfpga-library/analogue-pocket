Ambient Pocket

Generative ambient music, indefinitely: DRIFT, TIDES, HUSH and MOTHER.
The composer runs on the Pocket (PicoRV32 soft CPU) and plays live. A or Start starts / stops,
Left / Right changes mode, B reseeds, Up / Down density, L / R brightness, X cycles the visuals
(Full / Minimal / Off), Y shows the info box. Hold Select + Start for 2 s to lock the buttons
(Pocket lock). Supports sleep / wake and Memories. Settings are also in the core menu.
Based on the openFPGA core template (https://github.com/open-fpga/core-template).
