K3V GBA

Open-source Game Boy Advance FPGA core for Analogue Pocket.

Features include RTC, save states, fast-forward, button turbo, display filters,
rumble, and partial two-player link-cable support.

Source and issue tracker:
https://github.com/K3v-68/k3v-gba
