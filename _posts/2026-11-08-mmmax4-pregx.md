Konami pre-GX - Analogue Pocket openFPGA
By mmmax4, based on plasticbugs/Scott's Gaiapolis core.
Gaiapolis EAF / Mystic Warriors EAA / Violent Storm EAC / Metamorphic Force EAA.
Four playable profiles; Pocket validation scope documented in the repository.
Physical DIP switches and EEPROM Test/Service are separate.
Independent per-game saves; preserve existing .sav files.
https://github.com/mmmax4/openFPGA-Konami-PreGX
