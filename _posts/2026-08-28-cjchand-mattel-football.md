Mattel Football

An openFPGA core for the Analogue Pocket, reimplementing Mattel Electronic
Football (1977) from a B6100 CPU core built and verified against MAME.

Requires the game's ROM file, supplied by the user, at
Assets/mattel_football/common/mfootb.bin on the SD card.
