Toobin'
Atari Games, 1988

Ride an inner tube down the river,
dodging branches, alligators and
whirlpools. The whole board in
gateware: the 68010, the Toobin'
video, and the JSA I sound board
(6502, YM2151, POKEY).

ROMs are not included. Build the image
from your own MAME toobin romset with
mra_build.py and toobin.mra, and copy
it to Assets/toobin/common/toobin.rom

* D-pad: steer (paddles together)
* Y / X: left / right paddle forward
* L / R: left / right paddle back
* A / B / Start: throw (and start)
* Select: coin (two per player)
Settings and high scores save to
toobin.sav. Service mode: tick Service
Switch in the menu, then Reset Core.
Analogizer: in the menu; take any
cartridge out first.
