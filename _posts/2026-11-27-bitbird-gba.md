BitBird GBA 0.3.0

Play ROMs from the SD card, or insert
a cartridge and choose Play Cartridge.
Cartridge saves are written back to
the cart, with a backup on the SD card.

- Memories and sleep/wake
- Fast-forward
- Clock and playtime overlays
- Soft Colors
- Real-time clock support
