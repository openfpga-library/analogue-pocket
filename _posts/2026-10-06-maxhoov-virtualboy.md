Virtual Boy 1.0.0 - Pocket core
Original Virtual Boy RTL: Jamie Blanks.
384x224 native-rate video; selectable eye/anaglyph display.
8 KiB cartridge SRAM via openFPGA data slot 1.
Use a dock controller for complete dual-pad controls.
Prior builds: cartridge saves and menu pause tested on Pocket 2.7.
Savestates and sleep/wake support; hardware validation pending.
Snapshot format 2; MiSTer snapshots are incompatible.
No cheats or link support.
