Game.com for Analogue Pocket
Original FPGA core: Kitrinx. Pocket port: jakesjews.
Needs the 256 KB external BIOS at Assets/gamecom/common/boot.rom.
L + D-pad: move touch cursor; R or L + A: touch.
Start: Pause. Select: Game.com Menu.
When the Game.com is off, press A, B, X, Y or Start to turn it on.
