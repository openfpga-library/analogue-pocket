Dstroy is a fast maze-battle game about bombs, power-ups, monsters, and beautifully bad decisions. Break soft walls, collect bonuses, avoid your own explosions, and try to survive the chaos.

Dstroy was originally released for DOS by
Fully Bugged Software in 1995:
Nicolas Riviere
Andre Rieussec
Jerome Angelot
David Gallo

Modern Dstroy source port/remake by
Michael Doguet

Analogue Pocket port/remake by
HtheB

How To Play:
Adventure: Defeat the monsters and clear levels.
Battle: Be the last standing player.

Handheld mode supports 1 player normally.

2 Player handheld mode is also supported:
P1 moves with the D-Pad
and drops bombs with L.
P2 moves with A/B/X/Y
and drops bombs with R.
Select goes back.

Docked mode supports up to 4 players with external controllers.
