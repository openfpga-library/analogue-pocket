Atari Jaguar (cartridge games) for Analogue Pocket.

Requires the Jaguar boot ROM, not included:
  Assets/jaguar/common/jagboot.rom

Hold Select for the number pad:
  Y=1 Up=2 X=3 / Left=4 Start=5 Right=6 / B=7 Down=8 A=9 / L=* L+R=0 R=#
Tap Select for Option. X, L and R keys: Core Settings.

Ported from Jaguar_MiSTer (Torlus, ElectronAsh, Kitrinx, GreyRogue);
68000 by Nuked; Pocket modules by agg23; platform art by Spiritualized1997.
Pocket port by mmnbass (Michael Merida-Nicolich).
