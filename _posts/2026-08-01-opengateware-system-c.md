Sega System C / C-2
FPGA Compatible Gateware Core

Sega's System C (1989) and its
successor C-2 (1990) are 16-bit
JAMMA arcade boards built on
the Mega Drive architecture.
Designed as a cost-effective
arcade solution, they targeted
the puzzle, party, and casual
game market. The platform was
also known as "System 14."
